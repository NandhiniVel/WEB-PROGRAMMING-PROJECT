# 🐾 PawAdopt — Modern Pet Adoption & Rescue Network

A modern, highly-legible pet adoption platform featuring a sleek dark-violet SaaS aesthetic with glowing purple spotlights, glassmorphism, and crystal-clear typography.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Modern Violet Glow SaaS Aesthetic (Inspired by your reference UI)**:
   - Deep midnight violet space backdrop (`#090714` / `#120E24`).
   - Radiant purple spotlights and ambient glow (`#7C3AED` to `#6366F1`).
   - Crisp, crystal-clear typography in **Plus Jakarta Sans** & **Inter** (no serif or uncomfortable fonts).
   - Floating glass pill navigation and glowing purple action buttons (`rounded-full`).

2. **Full Pet Adoption Experience**:
   - Browse adoptable companions: **Dogs & Puppies 🐕**, **Cats & Kittens 🐈**, **Birds 🦜**, and **Small Animals 🐾**.
   - Filters by **Status** (`Ready to Adopt 💜`, `Inquiry Pending ⏳`, `Happily Adopted 🎉`), **Size** (`Small`, `Medium`, `Large`), and instant keyword search.
   - Medical clearance badges on each pet (`Vaccinated ✅`, `Spayed/Neutered ✅`, `Microchipped ✅`).
   - Personality traits & compatibility chips (`Good with Kids`, `Playful`, `Gentle`).

3. **Interactive Adoption Application Flow**:
   - Prospective families can click **"Apply to Adopt"** on any companion.
   - Clean, friendly modal collects housing situation, yard availability, pet experience, and message.
   - Applications are saved in real-time, automatically transitioning the listing to review.

4. **"List a Pet for Adoption" Creator**:
   - Shelters, rescues, or foster parents can register a rescue pet with photos, medical history, adoption fee, and bio.

5. **Rescue Shelters & Foster Locations Map**:
   - Dark-mode interactive Leaflet map with custom purple glowing pins.

6. **Printable Adoption Profile & Foster Flyer**:
   - Generate and print adoption posters with photo, medical verification, and tear-off tabs.
